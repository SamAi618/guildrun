What is Guildrun?

Guildrun is a single-player PvE roguelike autobattler developed by Leyline. Players recruit heroes, arrange their formation and combine abilities, items and relics before watching battles resolve automatically.

Every run offers different heroes and upgrades, encouraging players to adapt instead of following one fixed build. The Demo includes extensive progression, eight difficulty levels and Endless Mode, while a two-player cooperative mode is in development.
